Why choose FT AML

What FT AML actually does

A single platform covering the full anti-money-laundering lifecycle, not a patchwork of point tools.

Screen with confidence

Match customers against sanctions, PEP, and adverse media lists with configurable, explainable scoring — Watchlist Search for ad-hoc lookups, Batch and Delta Screening for ongoing coverage.

Monitor every transaction

Detect structuring, unusual transfers, and rapid movement with typologies and variables you build, test in Shadow mode, and publish yourself.

Investigate, don't just detect

Every alert has a clear path to a case, a disposition, and — where needed — a filed STR, with the full lifecycle timestamped for audit.

Triage without the noise

My Queue, All Alerts, and Admin View give analysts, supervisors, and MLROs exactly the visibility their role needs — nothing more.

One case, one audit trail

Alert Triggered → Investigator Review → Findings Documented → Disposition → MLRO Approval → Audit Record — the same lifecycle, every time.

Let AI draft the first pass

AI Manager pre-assembles screening history, transaction patterns, and prior cases into a draft summary — the analyst still makes the call.

Getting Started

The homepage every role lands on first. Shows live counts for open alerts, active cases, and screening volume, plus quick links into whichever module you use most — configurable per role so an analyst and an MLRO see different priorities.

Core Workflow

4 modules

Case Manager

Where a confirmed alert becomes a full investigation. Case Queues, a defined Case Lifecycle from review to sign-off, and an audit trail of every action taken along the way.

Explore

Alert Manager

Every alert from Screening or Monitoring lands here first. My Queue, All Cases, and Admin View give analysts, supervisors, and MLROs the visibility their role needs, with SLA countdowns on every row.

Explore

Customer Management

Every customer's profile in one record — KYC details, risk score, and full screening history. The single source of truth other modules (Case Manager, Name Screening) read from and write back to.

Explore

STR Listing & Reports

One row per filed Suspicious Transaction Report, linking back to the originating case and its transactions — plus the filter, export, and share views supervisors and regulators use on top of the same data.

Explore

AI & Screening

3 modules

AI Manager

Drafts a first-pass summary for incoming alerts and cases, then configures everything behind that drafting on two tabs: Prompt Config (the versioned instructions it follows) and Model Config (the model identity, API key, and Temperature it runs on, with full history and Restore).

Explore

Name Screening

Four ways in — Watchlist Search, Manual Screening, scheduled Batch runs, and Delta Screening for ongoing customers — with the matching weightages and severity bands behind every hit set once in its own Configuration area.

Explore

Automation

Configures how Name Screening and Transaction Monitoring alerts automatically become cases — which alert types feed case creation, and how they group together based on customer, case age, and status.

Explore

Monitoring & Data

2 modules

Transaction Monitoring

Typologies (rules) built from reusable Variables evaluate every transaction against your thresholds, versioned through Draft, Shadow, and Published states. Group By, Look Back, and Suppression shape exactly when — and how often — an alert should fire.

Explore

Data Ingestion

Scheduled Watchlist and TMS (transaction) ingestion jobs, with a clear job history so you can see exactly when data last landed and whether it validated cleanly.

Explore
