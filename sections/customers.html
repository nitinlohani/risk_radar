Customers

Navigation Homepage → FT AML → Customers

The single record of every party the platform knows — individuals and businesses alike. Every other module reads from and writes back to this record: a screening hit resolves against a customer here, a case investigates a customer here, and a filed STR names a customer here. This is where you look someone up and see their complete picture in one place.

Browsing and filtering customers

The main list covers every customer on the platform — often hundreds of thousands of records — so search and filtering are the primary way to work with it rather than scrolling.

KYC Level reflects how much verification is on file for that customer — Basic covers standard identity checks, Advanced means enhanced due diligence has been completed (typically required for higher-risk customers). Filtering by this narrows the list to exactly the verification tier you're reviewing.

Key features

  • Search by customer name or Customer ID
  • Filter by KYC Level — All, Basic, or Advanced
  • Refresh to pull the latest records, or Hide Filters to collapse the filter bar
  • Click any row to open that customer's full profile

Customer Profile

Opening a customer shows their core identity at the top — name, Customer ID, type (Individual or Business), segment, risk tier, KYC level, status, country, onboarding date, and sanctions screening result — plus declared monthly spend, source of funds, and linked accounts. Clicking More expands this into the complete KYC record: full Profile, Risk & Compliance (including PEP flag), Location, Business & Employment, and Financial Information.

How to use

  • Click More to see the complete KYC record; click Less to collapse it back to the summary header
  • Check PEP Flag and Risk Tier first when assessing why a customer needs closer attention
  • Linked Accounts are listed just below the header, with their own active/inactive status

Analytics, Transactions, and history tabs

Below the profile header, five tabs cover everything that's happened with this customer. Analytics is the default view — incoming versus outgoing activity, top counterparties, and transaction type breakdown, over a 1-day, 7-day, or 30-day window (with an optional Alert Window toggle to focus on activity around a specific alert).

Transactions lists every transaction tied to this customer, with filters for type, channel, price range, date, and sender/receiver ID — exportable as CSV for offline review.

Prior Cases / Alerts shows this customer's investigation history — every case and alert ever raised against them, so you can see at a glance whether this is a first-time hit or a repeat pattern.

Screening History lists every name-screening run this customer has been through — Delta (ongoing re-checks as watchlists update) and Batch (scheduled bulk re-screens) both appear here, each with its resulting alert score.

STR History lists any Suspicious Transaction Reports filed naming this customer, filterable by who filed it and when — an empty state here is common and simply means no STR has ever named this customer.

How to use

  • Switch between the 5 tabs — Analytics, Transactions, Prior Cases / Alerts, Screening History, STR History — to view a different slice of this customer's activity
  • Use each tab's own filters (date range, status, assignee) to narrow what's shown
  • An empty state (e.g. "No prior cases," "Oops! No data available") means nothing exists yet for that view — not an error
